ID: music-001
TITLE: One beat at a time
QUESTION: In 4/4, with the crotchet (quarter note) as one beat, how long is a minim (half note)?
A: Two beats
B: Half a beat
C: Four beats
ANSWER: A
EXPLANATION: A minim lasts twice as long as a crotchet. The beat unit matters when translating note values into beats.
EXAMPLE: Clap: crotchet, crotchet, minim. Count 1, 2, 3-4. The pattern fills one 4/4 bar.

ID: music-002
TITLE: The major-scale recipe
QUESTION: Which interval pattern builds a major scale from its tonic?
A: Tone, tone, semitone, tone, tone, tone, semitone
B: Seven consecutive semitones
C: Tone, semitone, tone, tone, semitone, tone, tone
ANSWER: A
EXPLANATION: The major pattern is W-W-H-W-W-W-H, where W is a whole tone and H is a semitone.
EXAMPLE: C major: C D E F G A B C. Check the semitones at E-F and B-C.

QUESTION: In 4/4, how many crotchet beats does a dotted minim (dotted half note) last?
A: Two
B: Four
C: Three
ANSWER: C
EXPLANATION: A dot adds half of the original note's duration. A two-beat minim gains one beat, making three.
EXAMPLE: A dotted minim plus one crotchet fills a 4/4 bar: 3 + 1 = 4 beats.

ID: music-006
TITLE: Sharps and flats
QUESTION: In standard Western notation, what does a sharp normally do to a note?
A: Raises it by one octave
B: Raises it by one semitone
C: Always makes it louder
ANSWER: B
EXPLANATION: A sharp changes pitch, not loudness. Its precise frequency depends on the tuning system, but the notated interval is one semitone.
EXAMPLE: On a piano, C-sharp is the next key above C. Volume instructions use other markings such as forte or piano.
