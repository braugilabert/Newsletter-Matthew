ID: neuroscience-001
TITLE: Dopamine beyond pleasure
QUESTION: Which description best captures dopamine's roles in the brain?
A: The sole neurotransmitter used for vision
B: A chemical that produces only pleasure
C: Movement, motivation, learning and reward-related signalling
ANSWER: C
EXPLANATION: Dopamine has several pathways and receptor types. Its functions cannot be reduced to a single emotion.
EXAMPLE: A reward arriving unexpectedly can change dopamine-cell firing in some circuits. Separately, nigrostriatal dopamine supports movement.
