ID: irish-001
TITLE: How are you?
QUESTION: Choose the ordinary Irish question meaning 'How are you?'
A: Cé hé sin?
B: Cá bhfuil tú?
C: Conas atá tú?
D: Cad is ainm duit?
ANSWER: C
EXPLANATION: Conas asks how. Cá asks where. Cé asks who. Conas atá tú? is a common standard form, with regional alternatives in everyday speech.
EXAMPLE: Mini-dialogue: Conas atá tú? Tá mé go maith, go raibh maith agat. Meaning: How are you? I am well, thank you.
