# Content sources and editorial notes

All quiz vignettes, distractors, explanations and worked examples were written for this project. Cases are fictional. Links provide background reading; they are not claims that a source has reviewed or endorsed the quiz bank.

## Neuropsychology and neuroscience

- [OpenStax: Introduction to Behavioral Neuroscience](https://openstax.org/details/books/introduction-behavioral-neuroscience): broad background for neural systems, methods, cognition and neurotransmission.
- [OpenStax: neurotransmitters made from amino acids](https://openstax.org/books/introduction-behavioral-neuroscience/pages/3-2-neurotransmitters-made-from-amino-acids): transmitter functions depend on pathways and receptors.
- [NIDCD: Aphasia](https://www.nidcd.nih.gov/health/aphasia): language syndromes, causes and limitations of simple localisation.
- [NCBI Bookshelf: Agnosia](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK493156/): recognition impairments and related clinical terminology.

Clinical items ask for the best explanation **within the information supplied**. They distinguish language, attention, visual fields, praxis and recognition, and avoid equating every function with one isolated brain spot. Epilepsy items include supporting EEG findings when lateralisation is relevant. Neurotransmitter items avoid labels such as dopamine = pleasure or oxytocin = universal trust.

## Psychotherapy

- [OpenStax Psychology 2e: Types of Treatment](https://openstax.org/books/psychology-2e/pages/16-2-types-of-treatment): overview of therapeutic traditions and learning principles.
- [NIMH: Psychotherapies](https://www.nimh.nih.gov/health/topics/psychotherapies): what psychotherapy involves and how approaches differ.
- [Beck Institute: History of CBT](https://cares.beckinstitute.org/about-cbt/history-of-cbt/): historical development of cognitive therapy.
- [Connecticut DMHAS: DBT](https://portal.ct.gov/dmhas/initiatives/evidence-based/dbt): acceptance, change and the origin of DBT.

Items teach terminology and conceptual distinctions. Illustrations of exposure, ERP and other clinical interventions explain the ideas rather than prescribe a treatment plan.

## Irish

- [Teanglann: dictionary, grammar and pronunciation](https://www.teanglann.ie/en/).
- [Teanglann: bí](https://www.teanglann.ie/en/fgb/b%C3%AD): present, past, future and habitual forms.
- [Foclóir: English–Irish dictionary](https://www.focloir.ie/): further word and phrase lookup.

Instructions are in English. Tasks use short practical sentences at approximately A2/B1, with standard forms and explanations in English. Regional variants are not automatically wrong. Pronunciation practice is directed to native audio rather than invented English phonetic spellings.

## Music

- [musictheory.net: Lessons](https://www.musictheory.net/lessons): note values, metres, intervals, scales, chords and inversions.

Pitch-distance questions state equal temperament where useful. Rhythmic questions specify the time signature and beat unit. Irish/British terminology is paired with American terminology where needed, for example crotchet / quarter note.

## Ireland’s News

- [RTÉ Ireland](https://www.rte.ie/news/ireland/).
- [The Journal](https://www.thejournal.ie/).
- [Irish Examiner Ireland](https://www.irishexaminer.com/news/).

The social justice editorial lens is explicit. Source headlines remain attributed and linked. Selection is automated from recent dated feeds; it does not claim an exhaustive or uniquely objective ranking of Ireland's most important events.

The reproducible preview snapshot was fetched from the three live RSS feeds on 20 November 2026. It contains original source headlines, direct article links and publication timestamps. Exact records are in `previews/news_snapshot.json`. The snapshot is only a preview input: live sends always fetch fresh RSS. The example archive does not invent older weeks of news.
